# ESPuino Handbook

Welcome to the handbook for **ESPuino** – the RFID-controlled audio player built on the ESP32.

This handbook brings the documentation together in one place. Discussion, announcements, and
support continue to happen on the [ESPuino forum](https://forum.espuino.de); this handbook is the
**reference source**.

!!! info "Focus of this handbook"
    This handbook describes the current [**Complete** board](hardware/complete.md). Its direct
    predecessor, the **mini4L**, only appears as a delta (chapter 16). Older development stages
    (early DIY builds, stripboard versions, carrier PCBs) are not covered.

!!! warning "State of this description: the `dev` branch"
    This handbook always describes the **current state of the `dev` branch** – in other words,
    whatever most recently landed in the development version. If you're running firmware from the
    `master` branch, a setting described here may therefore be missing or look different on your
    device. The [changelog](https://github.com/biologist79/ESPuino/blob/dev/changelog.md) tells you
    what was added when.
